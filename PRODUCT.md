# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Members of Dublin's Shia Muslim community: families checking daily prayer times and the Hijri calendar, parents opening the kids' corner (videos, madrasa books, quizzes), readers of duas and ziyarat, and a small set of admins who publish events, stories, reminders, and billboard ads from their phones. Used daily, mostly on mobile, often installed as a PWA.

## Product Purpose

A calm companion for prayer, supplication and community life for Ahlul Bayt Ireland. One place for accurate Dublin prayer times with azan alerts, the Islamic calendar with community and historical events, a supplication library with per-language translations, community classifieds, and admin-published announcements. Success is quiet daily usefulness: people open it, get what they need, and leave.

## Brand Personality

Serene, warm, devotional. The app should feel like dusk prayer time: quiet, unhurried, and dignified. Never gamified-loud (outside the kids' quiz), never corporate.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard aesthetics (stat tiles, cold grays, data-dense chrome).
- Loud Islamic-app clichés: neon green mosque silhouettes, heavy gold ornament, busy geometric-pattern wallpaper.
- Notification-heavy engagement patterns; the app must never nag.

## Design Principles

1. **Prayer first.** The next prayer time is the most important object in the product; it earns the richest visual treatment.
2. **One calm surface.** A single warm, consistent theme across all screens; no section may feel like a different app.
3. **Reverence for the texts.** Arabic is set large and beautiful (Noto Naskh Arabic); reading surfaces stay uncluttered.
4. **Admin is a tool, not a product.** The dashboard is plain, fast and predictable; polish budget goes to the member-facing screens.
5. **Offline is normal.** Everything meaningful works from cache; the design never depends on a network fetch to look right.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text ≥ 4.5:1 contrast on its surface; large display numerals ≥ 3:1.
- Adjustable reading text size (60%–150%) in the supplication reader; RTL support for Arabic, Urdu and Farsi content.
- Multi-language UI (English, Arabic, Hindi, Farsi, Urdu).
- Touch targets ≥ 30px; the app is operated one-handed on phones.
